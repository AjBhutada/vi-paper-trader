Rules

A Overnight futures: at 15:20 a limit buy 1 paisa below the last price (filled only if the market trades below it); if not filled by 15:25, market buy at the best ask. Sell at the next 09:15 pre-open auction price. As many lots as ₹ allows with a cushion. Switch to next month when fewer than 5 trading days are left before expiry.

B Gap-up auction short: a limit sell at yesterday's last price × 1.01 in the 09:00–09:08 pre-open; it fills only if the auction price is that high, and then at the auction price. Short 5× the account (max ₹50 lakh). Buy back with a limit 1 paisa below the price at 09:16; if not filled by 09:17, market buy at the best ask.

C Gap-up basket: the same idea as B on 13 stocks, at 2%: a limit sell at yesterday's last price × 1.02 in the pre-open, filled at the auction price. Up to 3 stocks a morning, taken in the priority order below; each short is at most 5% of what that stock usually trades in its opening minute (last 20 days), at most 10% of that morning's pre-open auction value at 09:06:30 (a big sell drags the auction price down), at most ₹50 lakh and at most 2× the Plan C account, and all together at most 5× (so 2× + 2× + 1×: the 2:2:1 rule). Buy back at 09:20 at the best ask, or at once if the stock trades 5% above our short price first (a disaster stop-loss, active from 09:15: stop orders are not allowed in the pre-open).
