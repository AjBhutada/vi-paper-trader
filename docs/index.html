How the three plans work

A Overnight futures: buys Vodafone Idea futures at 15:25 and sells them at the next morning's 09:15 opening.

B VI gap-up short: if Vodafone Idea opens 1% or more above yesterday's close, it short sells at the 09:15 opening and buys back at 09:16–09:17.

C 13-stock gap-up basket: if one of 13 chosen stocks opens 2% or more up, it short sells at the 09:15 opening (up to 3 stocks) and buys back at 09:20, or at once if the price runs 5% against it.

Each plan started with ₹5 lakh and keeps its profits. Live bot = acts in real time and sends its orders to the Upstox sandbox (pretend orders, real prices). Paper bot = works the same trades out afterwards from recorded prices.
